# India Market Reference

## Market Timings (IST)
- Pre-open: 9:00 AM – 9:15 AM
- Regular session: 9:15 AM – 3:30 PM
- Post-close: 3:40 PM – 4:00 PM

## Key Regulators
- **SEBI** — Securities and Exchange Board of India (market regulator)
- **RBI** — Reserve Bank of India (monetary policy, currency)
- **IRDAI** — Insurance regulator
- **PFRDA** — Pension fund regulator

## Key Economic Indicators to Track
- RBI Repo Rate (monetary policy)
- CPI Inflation (monthly)
- GDP Growth (quarterly)
- IIP (Industrial Production)
- FII/DII flows (daily)
- Rupee vs USD
- India VIX (fear index)

## Major Institutional Players
- **FII/FPI**: Foreign Institutional/Portfolio Investors (major market movers)
- **DII**: Domestic Institutional Investors (MFs, insurance companies)
- **Retail**: Individual investors
