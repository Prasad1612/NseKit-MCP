# Valuation Guide — Key Ratios & Benchmarks

## Valuation Ratios

### P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings)
- **Formula**: Market Price / EPS
- **Interpretation**: How much investors pay per ₹1 of earnings
- 🟢 Low P/E = potentially undervalued (or low growth)
- 🔴 High P/E = growth stock or overvalued
- Compare only within same sector

### P/B Ratio (Price-to-Book)
- **Formula**: Market Price / Book Value per Share
- **Interpretation**: Premium over net assets
- P/B < 1 = trading below book (may signal distress or value)
- Useful for: Banks, NBFCs, asset-heavy companies

### EV/EBITDA
- **Formula**: Enterprise Value / EBITDA
- **Use**: Capital structure-neutral valuation
- Better than P/E for debt-heavy companies
- Benchmark: <10x generally reasonable, >20x expensive

### ROE (Return on Equity)
- **Formula**: Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity × 100
- 🟢 >15% = Good | 🟡 10-15% = Average | 🔴 <10% = Poor
- High ROE + Low Debt = Quality business

### ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)
- **Formula**: EBIT / Capital Employed × 100
- ROCE > Cost of Capital = Value creation
- Target: >15% for most sectors

### Debt/Equity Ratio
- 🟢 <0.5 = Conservative | 🟡 0.5-1 = Moderate | 🔴 >1 = High leverage
- Exception: Banks and NBFCs naturally have high D/E

### Dividend Yield
- **Formula**: Annual Dividend / Market Price × 100
- >3% = Income stock | 1-3% = Moderate | <1% = Growth focus

## Quality Metrics

### Free Cash Flow (FCF)
- FCF = Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditure
- Positive FCF = business generates real cash
- FCF Yield = FCF / Market Cap (higher = better value)

### Interest Coverage Ratio
- **Formula**: EBIT / Interest Expense
- 🟢 >3x = Safe | 🟡 1.5-3x = Watch | 🔴 <1.5x = Danger

### Promoter Holding
- For Indian stocks: >50% promoter holding = confidence signal
- Declining promoter holding = potential warning sign
- Pledged shares > 30% = risk factor

## Growth Metrics
- Revenue CAGR (3-5 year): >15% = high growth
- EPS CAGR (3-5 year): >20% = strong earnings growth
- PEG Ratio = P/E / EPS Growth Rate (PEG < 1 = potentially undervalued)

## Sector-Specific Metrics

### Banking
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): >3% = good
- GNPA/NNPA: Lower is better
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR): >12% required
- CASA Ratio: >40% = strong deposit franchise

### IT Services
- Revenue Growth (CC terms)
- EBIT Margin: >20% = healthy
- Deal wins (TCV)
- Attrition rate: <20% preferred

### FMCG
- Volume Growth vs Price Growth
- Distribution reach
- Brand strength / market share

### Pharma
- ANDA filings and approvals (US)
- R&D as % of revenue
- USFDA compliance status
- Domestic formulations growth
